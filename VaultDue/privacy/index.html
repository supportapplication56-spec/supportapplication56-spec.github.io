Plain-English Summary: VaultDue does not operate any backend server, does not collect your personal records or documents, does not display advertisements, and does not sell or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays encrypted locally on your Android device.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

VaultDue is engineered under a strict Local-First & Zero-Knowledge architecture. We never receive, access, transmit, or store on any developer server:

  • Your subscriptions, billing amounts, currencies, renewal dates, price-change history, or cancellation notes;
  • Your warranties, purchase prices, serial numbers, or proof/receipt photos;
  • Your passport numbers, national IDs, residency/Iqama numbers, driver's licenses, vehicle registrations, or family profile labels;
  • Text you paste into the On-Device Smart Text Parser (processed 100% locally via deterministic rules — never sent to any cloud or AI API);
  • Your Vault PIN, biometric data (handled exclusively by Android OS BiometricPrompt), or .vdvault backup passphrases;
  • Advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, precise location, contacts, SMS messages, or behavioral analytics.

2. Optional In-App Purchase Processing (Google Play Billing)

VaultDue offers a free core tier and an optional one-time Pro unlock (vaultdue_pro_lifetime — single non-recurring payment, no subscription) processed exclusively through the official Google Play Billing Library.

  • VaultDue does not require sign-up or login and never sees your credit card, bank account, or billing address.
  • Google LLC processes payment transactions, fraud prevention, receipts, and purchase reactivation according to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Network access inside VaultDue is used solely by the Google Play Billing client to verify or reactivate your Pro entitlement with Google Play Store. VaultDue itself sends zero telemetry or cloud API requests.

4. Local Security & OWASP MASVS Controls

  • Hardware-Backed Encryption: Every vault record and proof image is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with keys managed inside Android Keystore (StrongBox hardware security module where available, or TEE), bound with record-specific Additional Authenticated Data (AAD).
  • Automatic EXIF & GPS Stripping: Attached images are re-encoded in volatile RAM prior to encryption so GPS coordinates, camera serials, and EXIF metadata are permanently removed.
  • PIN & Backup Cryptography: Vault PINs use salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with brute-force lockout protection; portable .vdvault backups use PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (210,000 iterations) + authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Screen & Backup Hardening: FLAG_SECURE blocks screenshots and task-switcher thumbnails by default; android:allowBackup="false" prevents unencrypted cloud backup of vault files; copied serial/document numbers are tagged EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE.

5. International Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Saudi & UAE PDPL)

Because all vault content resides exclusively on your device under your sole control, you can exercise every statutory right directly inside VaultDue with zero waiting period:

  • Access & Data Portability (GDPR Art. 15 & 20 / PDPL): Export your complete vault anytime as an encrypted .vdvault archive or human-readable CSV/JSON file under Security > Encrypted Backup & Data Export.
  • Rectification (GDPR Art. 16): Edit any record or replace any attachment anytime.
  • Erasure / Data Deletion (GDPR Art. 17 / Google Play Data Deletion Policy): Permanently delete all records, shred encrypted proof files, clear PIN hashes, and destroy the Android Keystore master key in one tap via Security > Cryptographically Wipe All Data, or uninstall the app.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California Residents): VaultDue does not sell or share personal information or sensitive personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and never discriminates against users exercising privacy rights.
  • No Cross-Border Transfers (GDPR Chapter V / Saudi PDPL Art. 29 / UAE PDPL): VaultDue transmits zero vault data outside your device.

6. User-Initiated Exports, PDFs, External Links & Calendar Actions

If you explicitly tap Share Proof, Export PDF Report, Add to Calendar, Open Official Cancel Page, or Export Plaintext CSV/JSON, Android passes only what you selected to the browser, calendar, or app of your choosing. Once exported or opened outside VaultDue, protection depends on the receiving service or website.

7. Children's Privacy

VaultDue is a personal finance and document utility intended strictly for users aged 13 and older (or 16 and older where required by local law, such as certain EU Member States). During onboarding, users confirm they meet the minimum age without submitting a birth date. Because VaultDue collects zero personal data and displays zero ads, no data from children is collected, stored, or profiled.

8. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as features or laws evolve. The latest version is always published at https://supportapplication56-spec.github.io/VaultDue/privacy/ and bundled offline inside the app. Contact: supportapplication56@gmail.com.
