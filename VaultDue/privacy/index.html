Plain-English Summary: VaultDue does not operate any backend server, does not collect your personal records or documents, does not display advertisements, and does not sell or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays encrypted locally on your Android device.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

VaultDue is engineered under a strict Local-First & Zero-Knowledge architecture. We never receive, access, transmit, or store on our servers:

  • Your subscriptions, billing amounts, currencies, renewal dates, or cancellation notes;
  • Your warranties, purchase prices, serial numbers, or proof/receipt photos;
  • Your passport numbers, national IDs, residency/Iqama numbers, driver's licenses, or family member names;
  • Text you paste into the On-Device Smart Text Parser (processed 100% locally via deterministic rules — never sent to any cloud or AI API);
  • Your Vault PIN, biometric data (handled exclusively by Android OS BiometricPrompt), or backup passphrases;
  • Advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, precise location, contacts, SMS messages, or behavioral analytics.

2. Optional In-App Purchase Processing (Google Play Billing)

VaultDue offers a free core tier and an optional one-time Lifetime Pro Unlock (vaultdue_pro_lifetime) processed exclusively through the official Google Play Billing Library.

  • VaultDue does not collect or see your credit card, bank account, or billing address.
  • Google LLC processes payment transactions, fraud prevention, purchase receipts, and purchase restoration according to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Network access inside VaultDue is used solely by the Google Play Billing client to verify or restore Pro entitlement with Google Play Store. VaultDue itself makes zero outbound API requests.

4. Local Security & OWASP MASVS Controls

  • Hardware-Backed Encryption: Every vault record and proof image is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with keys managed inside Android Keystore (StrongBox hardware security module where available, or TEE), bound with record-specific Additional Authenticated Data (AAD).
  • Automatic EXIF & GPS Stripping: Attached images are re-encoded in volatile RAM prior to encryption so GPS coordinates, camera serials, and EXIF metadata are permanently removed.
  • PIN & Backup Cryptography: Vault PINs and portable .vdvault backups use PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (210,000 iterations) with 256-bit random salts and authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Screen & Backup Hardening: FLAG_SECURE blocks screenshots and task-switcher thumbnails by default; android:allowBackup="false" and Android Data Extraction Rules prevent unencrypted cloud backup of vault files; clipboard copies of serial/document numbers are tagged EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE.

5. Your International Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Saudi & UAE PDPL)

Because all vault content resides exclusively on your device under your sole control, you can exercise every statutory right directly inside VaultDue with zero waiting period:

  • Right of Access & Data Portability (GDPR Art. 15 & 20 / PDPL): Export your complete vault anytime as an encrypted .vdvault archive or human-readable CSV/JSON file under Security > Encrypted Backup & Data Portability.
  • Right to Rectification (GDPR Art. 16): Edit any record or replace any attachment at any time.
  • Right to Erasure / Data Deletion (GDPR Art. 17 / Google Play Data Deletion Policy): Permanently delete all vault records, overwrite and unlink encrypted proof blobs, clear PIN hashes, and destroy the Android Keystore master key in one tap via Security > Cryptographically Wipe All Data, or uninstall the application from Android Settings.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California Residents): VaultDue does not sell or share personal information or sensitive personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not discriminate against users exercising privacy rights.
  • No Cross-Border Transfers (GDPR Chapter V / Saudi PDPL Art. 29 / UAE PDPL): VaultDue transmits zero user vault data outside your device.

6. User-Initiated Exports, PDFs & Calendar Actions

If you explicitly tap Share Proof, Export PDF Proof Dossier, Add to Calendar, or Export Plaintext CSV/JSON, you direct Android to pass that specific document to an app or storage location of your choosing. Once exported outside VaultDue, protection of those files depends on the receiving app or storage service.

7. Children's Privacy

VaultDue is a general personal finance and document organization utility intended for users aged 13 and older (or 16 and older where required by local law, such as in certain EU Member States). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
